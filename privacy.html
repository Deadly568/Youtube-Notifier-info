YouTube Notifier — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

1. Information Used by the Bot

YouTube Notifier uses configuration information needed to provide its notification service. The current configuration contains Discord server IDs, Discord notification channel IDs, and YouTube channel IDs.

2. What We Do Not Intentionally Store

Based on the bot's current configuration, YouTube Notifier does not intentionally store Discord user messages, usernames, passwords, or other user-profile information.

3. How Information Is Used

The configuration information is used to associate a configured YouTube channel with a configured Discord notification channel and to deliver the bot's notifications.

4. Third-Party Services

The bot relies on Discord and YouTube services and APIs to provide its functionality. Their handling of information is governed by their respective policies and terms.

5. Data Retention

Configuration information is retained only as needed to operate the configured notification service. If a server configuration is removed from the bot, that configuration can no longer be used for notifications.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect the bot's configuration. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the bot's functionality or data handling changes. The updated version will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please contact the bot owner through the support/contact method provided with the bot.
